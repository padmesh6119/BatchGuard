# BatchGuard: product spec

## Decision
A drug inspector has capacity to sample N batches this week. BatchGuard answers one question: **which batches first, and why.** The inspector decides. The system never blocks medicine and never declares a batch counterfeit.

## Two data layers, always labeled

| Layer | Source | Label | A negative label means |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, real | CDSCO central and state NSQ lists and spurious-drug lists, Jul 2025 to Aug 2026, 14 monthly workbooks | earlier manufacturer text reported again in the next month | no report next month; not that the medicine is safe |
| B, simulated | scan events generated on real reference batches | episode generated by a clone scenario | generated benign behavior; says nothing about chemical quality |

A persistent banner states this on every screen: *Layer A REAL, Layer B SIMULATED, a scan anomaly is not proof of counterfeit.*

## Three signals, kept separate (0 to 100 each)
1. **Historical risk (real):** percentile of the Layer A model's probability among manufacturer texts seen before the decision month. A manufacturer with no earlier record scores 0. That means "no history", not "safe".
2. **Scan anomaly (simulated):** declared rule over a six-hour window. One pack seen 100 km or more apart at a speed above 900 km/h scores 100. Ten or more scans of one pack within 5 minutes score 70. The trained scan model's probability is shown beside it. The rule drives alerts because the model did not beat it on held-out families.
3. **Evidence quality:** 0, 50 or 100 for one, two, or three or more asserted sources on one pack. Independence is not verified.

**Priority** comes from one of two untrained fusion policies, selected on the validation month only:
- weighted: `.50·history + .35·scan + .15·quality·alert`
- tiered: `alert ? 50 + scan/2 : history/2`

## Queue
- One row per product, batch and manufacturer-text candidate.
- Exact delivery retries (same `event_id`) are deduplicated and never alert.
- Capacity N is a maximum, not an obligation.
- Reason codes on every row, for example `CLONE_PATTERN_TRAVEL_REVIEW`, `REPEATED_PACK_BURST_REVIEW`, `PRIOR_NSQ_RECORDS=k`, `NO_EARLIER_MANUFACTURER_HISTORY` and `FOLLOWUP_SAMPLE_STATUS_UNKNOWN`.

## Contracts
- **ScanEvent:** exactly `event_id, pack_id, product, batch_no, manufacturer_key, event_time (UTC offset required), source_id, latitude, longitude, simulated`. Anything else is rejected.
- **Layer A features (7):** prior records, prior months, prior products, records last month, records in the last 3 months, months since last report, unresolved failure records. All are computed only from months before the target month.
- **Layer B features (9):** unique events, unique packs, maximum events per pack, maximum sources per pack, maximum pack distance, maximum pack speed, maximum events per pack in 5 minutes, observation span, retry fraction.
- **Excluded from features:** IDs, product names, scenario names and labels.
- **Splits:**
  - Layer A is chronological: train on target months up to May 2026, validate on June 2026, test on July and August 2026.
  - Layer B: pack identities never cross splits. The `delayed_sync` and `slow_copy` families appear in test only.
- **Model selection:**
  - Fixed small grids: logistic regression and depth-2/3 gradient boosting, in scikit-learn or Snap ML.
  - Validation AP decides the model; a tie keeps the simpler one.
  - The alert threshold maximizes validation F1.
  - The test split is read once per run.
- **Integrity:**
  - Processed data and model files are hash-checked before use, because unpickling runs code.
  - Models are bound to the source code of the feature functions.
  - The data build is atomic: a failed build keeps the previous output.

## Evaluation (all on held-out data, against baselines)
- **Layer A:** compared on AP and on later reports captured at a top-K manufacturer budget.
  - Baselines: legacy 60/25/15 index, most past records, most records in the last 3 months, tie-aware random.
  - Bootstrap 95% CI on the AP gain.
- **Layer B:** precision and recall of model vs rule, plus alert rate per scenario family (seen and held-out).
- **Fusion:**
  - Unique-batch queues for each test month, built through the live ranking code.
  - Only batches published before that month enter its queue; a later batch would be selected on the outcome.
  - Measured: capture of simulated clones and of real recurring-manufacturer batches at N = 10, 25, 50, 100, 150.
- **Platform:**
  - The same workload hash runs on laptop and on LinuxONE: per-scan decision p50/p99, events/s, queue rebuild time.
  - Acceleration is claimed only if measured.

## Dashboard (`app/app.py`)
- Banner; capacity slider; the stream of arriving simulated scans (step through it).
- Ranked queue with the three signals, priority, model probabilities and reasons.
- Batch evidence: scan timeline, map of synthetic locations, packs and sources, real manufacturer history with source files, and published reports of the exact batch.
- Inspector decision log.
- Tabs: Evaluation, Platform (laptop and LinuxONE), Data quality.

## Not claimed
- Medicine safety probability.
- Real counterfeit-detection accuracy.
- Verified manufacturer identity.
- Source independence.
- Publication-time ordering: collection month is used as a proxy.
- Hypothetical flooding or attack hardening: deferred.
